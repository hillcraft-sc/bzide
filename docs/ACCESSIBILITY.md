# Accessibility in Bzide

Bzide is built to work with the accessibility features in iOS. You can sign in, browse your library, search, play music and control playback using any of the features below. If something doesn't work for you, please tell us (see Contact on the Support page).

## VoiceOver

- Every button says what it does, including the icon buttons in Now Playing and the mini player.
- Headings are announced as headings, so you can jump between sections with the rotor. Decorative pictures are skipped.
- The progress bar reads the time, such as "0:05 of 3:36". Swipe up or down on it to move 15 seconds forward or back.
- Synced lyrics are read line by line. Double-tap a line to jump the song there.
- Messages that appear on screen, such as one saying a song was added to a playlist, are read aloud.
- Color choices in Settings say their name and whether they're chosen.

## Easy to tap

Every button in Bzide has a tap area of at least 44 by 44 points, Apple's minimum, even where the icon you see is smaller, so you don't need to hit an icon exactly.

## Voice Control

Every button has a name you can say, such as "Tap Play", "Tap Next" or "Tap Favorite". Say "Show names" to see them, or "Show numbers" to tap anything by number.

## Larger Text

Bzide follows your text size (iPhone Settings > Display & Brightness > Text Size, or Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text Size > Larger Text).

- Lists, headings and lyrics grow with the text.
- At the largest sizes, layouts rearrange so text isn't squeezed or cut off: buttons move under their titles instead of beside them.
- Playback controls grow up to the largest standard size, and stay a size that fits on screen beyond it.

## Dark mode and themes

Bzide follows iOS's light and dark modes. You can also choose a theme in Settings > Appearance. Most are dark, including Catppuccin Mocha, Gruvbox Dark, Nord, Dracula and Tokyo Night.

## Color isn't the only signal

Nothing in Bzide is shown by color alone:

- Favorites show a filled heart. Ratings show a face (Meh, Like or Love) or a number of stars, and the Rate button's star is filled once something is rated.
- The song that's playing has a speaker icon in the queue.
- "EQ" and "EQ unavailable" are written out in Now Playing.
- When you've chosen different lyrics with **Wrong lyrics?**, its button shows a checkmark as well as changing color.

## Contrast

Bzide uses the system's text colors, which meet Apple's contrast guidelines. With Increase Contrast on (iPhone Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text Size), lyric lines that aren't playing are brighter and never blurred.

## Reduced motion

With Reduce Motion on (iPhone Settings > Accessibility > Motion):

- Lyrics move to the current line without bouncing or zooming.
- The visualizer doesn't start on its own. It shows "Reduce Motion Is On" and starts only if you tap **Start Anyway**.

To remove the visualizer completely, turn off **Settings > Visualizer**. Its button then disappears from Now Playing.

## Captions and audio descriptions

Bzide has no video, so captions and audio descriptions don't apply. For many songs, Bzide can show synced lyrics, from your server or (if you turn it on) from LRCLIB. See Lyrics in the user guide.
