## Description (2,105 of 4,000)

Bzide is a music player for your own Navidrome server. Your library, your files, no subscription, with playback built to just work.

NO SERVER YET? Tap Try the Demo on the sign-in screen to explore a sample library hosted by the Navidrome project. No account needed.

PLAYBACK YOU CAN TRUST
• Keeps playing with the screen locked and in the background
• Gapless playback; the next song is ready before the current one ends
• Recovers when you move between Wi-Fi and cellular or lose signal for a moment
• Pauses properly for calls, Siri and unplugged headphones
• Your queue and place are saved, so it picks up where you left off
• Continue a queue you started on another device

BUILT FOR THE CAR AND THE LOCK SCREEN
• CarPlay with Recents, your Library, Radio and downloads
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, plus a Control Center button to favorite the song playing
• Siri (with Apple Intelligence): "Play Miles Davis in Bzide"
• Full lock screen and Control Center controls

OFFLINE
• Download albums, playlists or single songs
• Downloaded playlists stay up to date
• Choose streaming and download quality separately for Wi-Fi and cellular

YOUR LIBRARY, YOUR WAY
• Artists, albums, genres, playlists and fast search with filters
• Favorites and ratings that sync with your server
• Random Mix, Mix My Cuts (your best-rated songs) and endless Spin Off from any song or artist
• Sleep timer and ten-band equalizer
• Synced, word-by-word lyrics
• Internet radio, with Spin the Dial to discover stations
• A full-screen music visualizer with twelve styles that moves to the beat
• Themes: Catppuccin, Gruvbox, Nord, Dracula, Tokyo Night, and your choice of accent color

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your login stays in the iPhone's Keychain, and your password is never sent. Sign in with an API key if your server supports it.
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics
• Anything that talks to another service (lyrics lookup, station search and so on) is off until you turn it on

Bzide works with Navidrome and other Subsonic-compatible servers. It is not affiliated with Navidrome. The source code is available under the GPL-3.0 license.
